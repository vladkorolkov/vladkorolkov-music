# Tech Lead

I work as Tech Lead & Software Architect at <a href="https://dbi.ru/" class="external" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DBI</a> (since 2022).

I design, develop and maintain high-load systems for airlines. As tech lead and architect my responsibilities include task decomposition, team task planning, code review, estimation, client communication and release delivery.

## Revenue Management System

A high-load microservices system for a Russian airline. It analyzes internal data, market saturation and demand to calculate the optimal price for a flight or service. It allows managing pricing across all distribution channels from a single dashboard and generating reports.

I worked on microservices development and support, PostgreSQL query optimization, scaling, refactoring and introducing architectural solutions. Stack: .NET, C#, EF Core, Go, Docker, Kubernetes, RabbitMQ, Grafana, Loki, Prometheus, GitLab CI.

## Online Flight Board

A system that displays flights for staff at check-in desks and other workplaces.

I worked as a fullstack developer: support, React interface improvements, query optimization, refactoring and tests. Stack: .NET, C#, EF Core, React, TypeScript, Oracle / PostgreSQL.

## Notification Services

A group of services for staff notifications (including pilots), integrations with government agencies and international organizations.

I improved and maintained background services, SMS provider integrations and file processing. Stack: .NET, C#, EF Core, PostgreSQL.
