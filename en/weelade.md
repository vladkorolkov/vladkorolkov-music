# Weelade

Under this name I occasionally create and release electronic music.

My spontaneous experiment — a live mix of a sermon and tracks by Photek from the album Modus Operandi. I think they work together in an interesting way. I decided not to publish this track on music services because there is no way to contact the music rights holder. So let’s keep it as underground as possible — here is a link to Yandex Disk. You can listen to it, and you can even download and use the file as you please.

## Collaborative album

Back in the already distant COVID times, I had the chance to make a spontaneous drum and bass album together with writer, philosopher, and musician Alexey Solovyov.

Alexey once wrote to me with a proposal to record guitar parts for one of his tracks, and before we knew it our collaboration grew into a full album project — we moved from online to offline, into the studio. We composed, experimented, recorded, mixed, and talked about philosophy during the breaks.

It was spontaneous, pure creativity — without grand plans, attempts to court recommendation algorithms, or anything of the sort.

So I’m simply sharing it with you. Link to streaming services: <a href="https://zvonko.link/1011EE0" class="external" target="_blank" rel="noopener">zvonko.link/1011EE0</a>
