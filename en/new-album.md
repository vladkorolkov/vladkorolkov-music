# New Album

<img src="https://vladkorolkov-music.ru/images/album-cover-new.jpg?v=2" alt="Otzvuki album cover" class="album-cover">

Original music by Rostov-on-Don jazz guitarist Vlad Korolkov. “Otzvuki” is contemporary jazz with elements of post-rock and atonal passages, performed together with some of the finest musicians from southern Russia. The album is both a musical abstraction and an attempt to process the experiences of different events preserved as echoes in sound.

The album was recorded live at Esse jazz club in Rostov-on-Don in 2022.

Saxophone — Yuri Krasilnikov  
Bass guitar — Oleg Solomko  
Drums — Alexander Bozhenko  
Sound engineer — Andrey Pilipenko
